Every attacker, one lane

Does fail2ban's 10-minute default ban keep attackers away, or does it take 12 hours? Each attacker caught by fail2ban's rule (five failed logins in ten minutes) gets an arm from Flagsmith, is banned on every server for that long, and is watched for a return 12 to 24 hours later, after every ban has ended.
